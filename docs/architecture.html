ictlib · system schematic

ICT concept-detection library

A dependency-light Python engine that turns raw candles into the building blocks of ICT price analysis. The primitive layer detects; everything downstream consumes — a scanner, a chart, and (next) a backtester all read the same Analysis snapshot instead of re-deriving it.

Primitive → rule (from the ICT knowledge library)

swing / structure

structure.py

n-bar fractal pivots; a close beyond the last opposite swing is a break — continuation (BOS) or first reversal (CHoCH).

H_n > H_{n±1..w}

mss

structure.py

The reversal-grade break: a CHoCH that carries displacement and leaves a fair value gap in the breaking move.

CHoCH + displacement + FVG

pd_arrays

pd_arrays.py reference frame

The dealing range (recent LTH/LTL) and its equilibrium; classifies every price and PD array as premium (sell-side) or discount (buy-side). Longs originate at a discount, shorts at a premium.

EQ = (LTH+LTL)/2 · |depth|=.79 → OTE

displacement

displacement.py

The filter that separates real PD arrays from noise — a wide, dominant, one-directional candle.

body≥1.5×avg · body/range≥.70

fvg

fvg.py tracks mitigation

Three-candle imbalance; midpoint is the consequent-encroachment entry. Marked mitigated once price rebalances it.

bull: L_{n+1} > H_{n-1}

order_blocks

order_blocks.py tracks mitigation

Last opposite-colour candle before a structure-breaking displacement; its body is the entry zone, midpoint the mean threshold.

last opposite → displacement → BOS

breaker_blocks

breaker_blocks.py tracks retest

An order block that failed and flipped polarity — price closed through the body against it, with displacement. The zone now defends the new direction on retest.

close through OB body + displacement

rejection_blocks

rejection_blocks.py

A long-wick rejection at a key level; the rejected wick (not the body) is the zone and the SL anchor.

wick ≥ 60% of range · at a level

imbalance

imbalance.py tracks mitigation

Volume imbalance — a body-vs-body gap between consecutive candles. A softer FVG-like reference; conviction stacks when it nests in an FVG.

O_n ≠ C_{n-1} (wicks may overlap)

liquidity

liquidity.py tracks swept

BSL/SSL pools at swings, EQH/EQL clusters within tolerance, and the sweep that runs them: wick through + close back + 60% wick.

high>lvl · close<lvl · wick≥.6

killzones

killzones.py

NY-clock windows — Asia, London, NY AM/PM — plus silver-bullet and macro sub-windows. DST-aware via the tz database.

America/New_York → window

sessions

sessions.py

The NY-time session map (Asia / London / NY AM / Lunch / PM / London Close); each session's high and low are resting liquidity.

session_of(t) · session_range()

asian_range

asian_range.py

The Asia range London delivers from — bounds, which side the Judas swing sweeps, and 0.5–2× extension targets.

[asian_low, asian_high] · n× projections

ipda

ipda.py

The 20/40/60 trading-day lookback highs and lows — the untaken extremes price is drawn toward. Feeds the draw-on-liquidity set.

20 / 40 / 60-day high & low

ote

ote.py

Optimal Trade Entry band on a measured leg; the 0.62–0.79 retracement is the entry-depth confluence.

retrace ∈ [0.62, 0.79]
